Review: The Google Pixel 9 Pro

Released as a powerhouse for mobile photography and AI integration, the Pixel 9 Pro continues to set the gold standard for Android flagships. With its gorgeous display, buttery-smooth 120Hz refresh rate, and deep Gemini AI capabilities, it handles multitasking effortlessly.

The battery life has also seen a massive improvement, easily lasting a full day of heavy usage. If you love clean software and top-tier photos, this phone is hard to beat.

Review: Sony WH-1000XM5 Headphones

When it comes to active noise cancellation (ANC), the Sony WH-1000XM5 remain undisputed kings. Whether you are working in a bustling coffee shop or flying across the country, these over-ear headphones completely silence the world around you.

They offer stellar audio fidelity with custom EQ support through the Sony app, plus an impressive 30-hour battery life. Comfort-wise, the lightweight headband and soft earcups make all-day listening a breeze.
